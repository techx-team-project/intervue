INTERVUE EMPLOYER SERVICE AND DATA EXCHANGE AGREEMENT
Version v2.1

1. Nature of Platform Services
InterVue operates as an AI-assisted recruitment infrastructure connecting validated technology
enterprises with candidate talent. Final hiring decisions rest exclusively with the Employer.

2. Employer Warranties
The Employer represents and warrants that all corporate information, business licences, job
listings, and hiring conditions posted are authentic, lawful, and accurately reflect genuine
employment openings.

3. AI Assistance and Transparency
AI tools provided on InterVue (including CV parsing, match scoring, and interview question
suggestions) serve as analytical aids. Employers agree to review recommendations with human
judgement and to avoid automated discriminatory hiring practices.
