INTERVUE DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT WITH CANDIDATES
(Pursuant to Decree No. 13/2023/ND-CP on Personal Data Protection)
Version v2.1

1. Purpose of Processing
The Employer commits to collecting, storing, and processing candidate personal data (including
Curriculum Vitae, contact details, work history, education, and screening responses) solely for the
legitimate purpose of recruitment and evaluation for positions posted on the InterVue platform.

2. Scope and Retention
- Candidate data shall be retained strictly during the active recruitment cycle and the subsequent
  retention period required by labour laws.
- Data shall not be transferred, disclosed, or commercialised to any unauthorised third parties
  without explicit consent.

3. Candidate Rights
Candidates retain the right to be informed, to review, to request rectification, or to request
erasure of their submitted application data in accordance with statutory privacy provisions.

4. Confidentiality and Security
The Employer implements rigorous technical and organisational security measures to protect candidate
records against unauthorised access, destruction, or disclosure.
