# Candidate Identity Verification and eKYC Policy

## Overview of Candidate Identity Verification (eKYC)
To build a trusted, authentic IT recruitment environment and protect employers against ghost candidates or fake applications, InterVue requires all candidates to complete citizen identity verification (eKYC) before submitting job applications:
- Mandatory Requirement: Unverified candidates can search jobs and manage CVs, but must complete identity verification to submit applications or message recruiters.
- Verification Badge: Once approved, a verified badge (green checkmark) appears on the candidate's profile and applications, increasing trust with employers.
- Zero Application Bias: Biometric and identification details are never exposed to recruiters; hiring managers only see the verification confirmation status.

## Required Documents and Verification Steps
Candidates verify their identity in two straightforward steps (xác thực danh tính eKYC qua CCCD và ảnh chân dung selfie):
- Step 1: Upload Citizen Identity Card (CCCD)
  - Clear, legible photos of both the front and back of the valid Vietnamese Citizen Identity Card (CCCD / Thẻ căn cước).
  - Images must not be cut off at the corners, blurry, or obscured by excessive lighting glare.
- Step 2: Portrait Selfie Verification
  - A real-time front-facing portrait photograph of the candidate's face.
  - The photo must match the identity photo on the CCCD without heavy digital filters, sunglasses, or face masks.

## Review Process and Application Limits
- Review Turnaround: Verification requests are reviewed by platform administrators within 24 business hours.
- Status Notifications: Candidates receive instant email and in-app notifications when their verification is approved or rejected.
- Retrying Rejected Submissions:
  - If a submission is rejected, the system displays specific rejection reasons (e.g., blurry image, obscured corners, mismatched selfie).
  - Candidates are permitted up to 5 verification attempts.
  - If a candidate reaches 5 unsuccessful attempts, they can request manual assistance from customer support.

## Data Privacy, Security, and Compliance
InterVue treats all identity documents with strict confidentiality in compliance with Vietnam Decree 13/2023/ND-CP on Personal Data Protection:
- Storage Encryption: CCCD images and biometric selfie data are stored in a private, encrypted storage bucket inaccessible via public URLs.
- Access Restrictions: Platform staff access identity records strictly on a need-to-know basis during manual review audits.
- Deletion on Account Closure: All identification and biometric records are permanently deleted upon account erasure following the 7-day grace period.
