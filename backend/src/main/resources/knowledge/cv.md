# Curriculum Vitae (CV) Management and AI Feedback

## CV Upload Specifications and Rules
Candidates can store, manage, and designate multiple CVs for different IT specializations on InterVue:
- Supported Format: Exclusively Adobe PDF files (.pdf). Scanned image-only PDFs or password-protected PDFs are rejected.
- Size and Page Limits: Maximum file size of 5 MB and maximum length of 5 pages.
- Naming and Default CV: Candidates can assign custom names to uploaded CVs and select a default CV used for one-click job applications.

## CV Storage Quotas by Plan
The number of active CVs an account can store simultaneously depends on the user tier:
- Standard (Free) Tier: Up to 3 active CVs.
- Education Tier: Up to 5 active CVs.
- Pro Tier: Up to 10 active CVs.
- Premium Tier: Up to 20 active CVs.

Submitted Application Immutability: When you apply for a job, InterVue captures an immutable snapshot of your CV at the time of submission. Deleting a CV from your profile management page will never alter or remove your CV from active recruiter applications, and archived application snapshots do not count against your active storage quota.

## Confidentiality and Access Control
Uploaded CVs are strictly private:
- Search engines and unauthorized external parties cannot crawl or view your CVs.
- Only the candidate and authorized hiring managers of job posts to which the candidate explicitly submitted an application have permission to inspect or download the CV.

## AI CV Review and Feedback Levels
InterVue provides intelligent AI evaluation to help candidates refine their CVs before submitting applications:

### Level 1: General CV Audit
Analyzes structural integrity, formatting readability, essential sections (contact, experience, skills, education), clear cause-and-effect descriptions, quantifiable achievements, and consistency of dates and spelling.

### Level 2: Target Position Review
Evaluates the CV against the candidate's declared target role in their profile (e.g., Frontend React Developer). Identifies whether key industry-expected technologies are backed by evidence in past projects and assesses whether descriptions align with the target seniority level.

Usage limits: Level 1 and Level 2 reviews share a combined daily limit of 3 free evaluations per day, resetting at 00:00 Vietnam Time. Re-evaluating an identical, unchanged CV returns cached feedback without consuming daily quota.

### Level 3: Job Posting Compatibility Check
Performs a deep comparison between the candidate's CV and the explicit technical requirements of a specific active job post. Provides point-by-point matching analysis, highlights missing keyword evidence, and suggests targeted emphasis. Level 3 checks are premium paid evaluations accessible via the "Check Compatibility" button on job detail pages.

## AI Review Ethical Standards
- Transparency: Every feedback item provides a concrete snippet quote from the CV, explains the diagnosed issue, and suggests actionable wording improvements.
- Integrity: AI never suggests adding fictitious work history, fabricated credentials, or false technologies.
- Anti-Discrimination: AI models never evaluate or factor in candidate age, gender, photograph, hometown, or KYC personal records.
