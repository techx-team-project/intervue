# AI-Powered Features and Candidate Tools

## Overview of Artificial Intelligence on InterVue
InterVue leverages advanced language models (LLMs) and vector embeddings to empower tech professionals and hiring managers. AI operates strictly as an intelligent assistant; it never makes automated decisions to reject or disqualify applicants (AI chỉ đóng vai trò trợ lý thông minh, không tự động loại bỏ hồ sơ ứng viên; quyết định phỏng vấn và tuyển dụng hoàn toàn thuộc về con người).

## AI Profile Sync from CV
Candidates can save time creating or updating their profile by allowing AI to parse their PDF CV (Đồng bộ hồ sơ từ CV bằng AI):
- Empty Profile Auto-Fill: For new accounts without profile information (hồ sơ trống), AI extracts headline, bio, years of experience, technical skills, work history, educations, certifications, and languages, pre-filling everything for one-click confirmation (xác nhận toàn bộ).
- Existing Profile Smart Diff: For profiles with existing data, the system performs a field-by-field comparison (so sánh dữ liệu cũ và mới) displaying "Old Value" vs "New Value" alongside selective checkboxes. Candidates choose exactly which fields to update or preserve.
- Unreadable CV Diagnosis: If a CV cannot be processed (e.g., scanned image-only PDF without OCR text layer, password-encrypted PDF, corrupted file, or exceeding 5 MB / 5 pages), the system displays the specific diagnosis (lý do cụ thể) and offers direct navigation to manual profile editing (nhập thủ công).

## AI Job Recommendations with Explanations and Nudge
InterVue recommends IT job openings customized to each candidate's background:
- Content and Skill Scoring: Evaluates candidate skills, desired job title, experience level, and preferred location against active job descriptions using intelligent matching algorithms.
- Fit Reason for Every Job: Each recommended job includes a concise one-sentence explanation highlighting why it matches the candidate (e.g., matching React and Node.js skills, aligned seniority level).
- Empty Profile Fallback and Nudge: If a candidate has not completed their profile (hồ sơ mới hoặc chưa có thông tin), the system displays the newest trending tech opportunities (việc làm mới nhất) accompanied by an encouraging nudge banner (nhắc nhở hoàn thiện hồ sơ) prompting them to complete key profile sections for personalized accuracy.

## AI CV Review and Audit Levels
Candidates can submit their CV for intelligent multi-level evaluation:
- Level 1: General Structure & Readability: Audits formatting clarity, essential sections, quantifiable impact metrics, and date consistency.
- Level 2: Target Role Alignment: Assesses whether the CV effectively demonstrates required technologies and competencies for the candidate's declared target role.
- Level 3: Job Description Compatibility Check: Compares the candidate's CV point-by-point against a specific active job post to highlight strengths, keyword gaps, and suggested improvements.
- Usage Quota: Level 1 and Level 2 offer 3 free daily audits per account. Level 3 is a premium evaluation available on job detail pages.

## AI Cover Letter Generation
Candidates can generate tailored, professional application cover letters for specific job posts:
- Personalized Drafting: Combines the candidate's default CV work experience with the target job's specific technical requirements.
- Professional Tone: Produces an articulate letter emphasizing relevant technical achievements, problem-solving skills, and cultural fit.
- Full Editing Control: Candidates can review, edit, and fine-tune the drafted letter before final submission.

## AI Platform Support Chatbot and Agent Tools
The InterVue Support Chatbot assists users across all platform pages with context-aware intelligence:
- Agent Tools: The assistant can execute live actions on behalf of the user, including `search_jobs` (finding jobs by skill, level, location), `get_company_info` (retrieving employer profiles), `get_my_applications` (checking personal application status), `match_my_cv` (compatibility analysis), and `draft_cover_letter` (writing application letters).
- Context-Aware Help: When browsing a specific job or company page, the chatbot automatically understands the context and offers relevant actions like matching your CV or drafting a cover letter for that exact job.
- Query Quotas: 10 queries per hour for unauthenticated guests (by IP), and 30 queries per day for authenticated users.
