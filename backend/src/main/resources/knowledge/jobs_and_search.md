# Job Search, Nearby Jobs, and Company Following

## IT Job Search and Smart Filtering
InterVue provides comprehensive search capabilities tailored for software engineers and tech talent:
- Keyword & Technology Matching: Search across job titles, technical skill tags (e.g., Java, React, Node.js, Python, Golang, AWS, Kubernetes), and company names.
- Multi-dimensional Filters: Filter by seniority level (Intern, Fresher, Junior, Middle, Senior, Lead, Manager), employment type (Full-time, Part-time, Contract), and work format (On-site, Hybrid, Remote).
- Salary Transparency: Filter by minimum and maximum salary ranges with transparent VND and USD salary displays.

## Jobs Near Me and Interactive Map Search
Candidates can discover tech opportunities closest to their living or working areas using the interactive map at `/jobs/near-me`:
- Interactive Leaflet Map: Displays job vacancies as location pins plotted directly on an interactive map. Clicking a pin opens a job preview card with company details and salary.
- Geolocation Detection: Candidates can allow browser geolocation to instantly center the map on their current GPS coordinates, or enter a custom address using the location picker.
- Distance Radius Filter: Adjust commuting radius dynamically across 5 km, 10 km, 25 km, and 50 km to find nearby technology offices.
- Directions and Commute: View office address details and estimated distance directly from your designated location.

## Saving Jobs for Later Application (Lưu việc làm)
- One-Click Bookmark: Click the bookmark icon on any job card or job detail page to save the listing to your personal collection (lưu việc).
- Saved Jobs Dashboard: Access all bookmarked opportunities at `/candidate/saved` to review deadlines, compare compensation, and apply when ready.
- Application Status Synchronization: Once you submit an application to a saved job, the system automatically moves it to your active applications tracker.

## Following Hiring Companies and Job Alerts (Theo dõi công ty và thông báo việc làm)
Candidates can follow top technology employers to stay informed about new openings:
- Follow Company Action: Click the "Follow" button (theo dõi công ty) on any verified company profile page.
- Instant Job Notifications: Whenever a followed company publishes a new job posting, followers receive in-app notifications and email alerts (thông báo việc làm mới) with direct links to apply.
- Managing Followed Companies: View and manage all followed tech companies in the "Followed Companies" tab under `/candidate/saved`.
