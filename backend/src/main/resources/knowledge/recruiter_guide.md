# Recruiter Guide: Company Verification, Job Posting, and Candidate Management

## Company Registration and Employer Verification
To protect job seekers and maintain verified employer standards, all hiring organizations must complete company verification:
- Required Credentials:
  - Official Business Registration Certificate (Giấy phép đăng ký kinh doanh).
  - Corporate email address matching the company's verified domain.
  - Official company phone number verified via SMS OTP.
- Admin Review Process: Platform administrators verify company legal status within 24 working hours (xét duyệt trong 24 giờ làm việc).
- Verified Employer Badge: Once verified, the company profile displays an official verification badge, boosting job post credibility and applicant response rates.

## Publishing IT Job Postings
Verified recruiters can publish tech job openings with comprehensive criteria:
- Technical Specifications: Specify target job title, required tech stack tags (e.g. Java, React, Python, Go, Docker), and seniority level.
- Compensation & Benefits: Transparent salary ranges (VND or USD), insurance, bonus structures, and flexible perks.
- Work Format & Location: Choose between On-site, Hybrid, or 100% Remote. For physical or hybrid positions, enter the office address and interactive map coordinates (latitude and longitude) to enable the "Jobs Near Me" feature.

## Candidate Pipeline and Application Review
Recruiters manage job applicants through an intuitive recruitment pipeline:
- Application Stages:
  1. APPLIED: Candidate submitted an application with their CV snapshot.
  2. IN_REVIEW: Hiring team is actively assessing the candidate's qualifications.
  3. INTERVIEW: Candidate is invited to one or more interview rounds.
  4. OFFER: Formal job offer extended to the candidate.
  5. HIRED: Candidate accepted the offer and joined the company.
  6. REJECTED: Candidate application declined with polite automated status notification.
- CV Snapshot Integrity: When an application is submitted, InterVue captures an immutable snapshot of the candidate's CV at that exact moment. Deleting or editing the CV later from candidate settings never alters existing applications.

## Ethical Hiring Standards and Non-Discrimination
InterVue upholds fair and transparent recruitment practices:
- Objective Evaluation: AI summaries and compatibility scores focus strictly on technical qualifications and experience.
- Protected Personal Data: AI models never assess or display candidate age, gender, marital status, or identification photos during matching, ensuring equal opportunity for all applicants.
