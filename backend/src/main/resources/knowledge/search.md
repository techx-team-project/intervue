# Job Search and Exploration on InterVue

## Public Job Search Capabilities
Candidates and guests can explore thousands of tech job opportunities across Vietnam and remote markets:
- Keyword and Title Search: Search by specific job titles (e.g., Golang Engineer, AI Specialist) or corporate brand names.
- Multi-dimensional Filtering:
  - Working Mode: On-site, Hybrid, Remote, or Flexible.
  - Contract Type: Full-time, Part-time, Contract, or Internship.
  - Seniority Level: Intern, Fresher, Junior, Middle, Senior, Lead, or Manager.
  - Salary Range: Minimum to maximum monthly compensation, or posts with negotiable salaries.
  - Working Location: Filter by major tech hubs including Ho Chi Minh City, Hanoi, Da Nang, Can Tho, or Remote.

## Jobs Near You (Location-Based Map Search)
InterVue offers an interactive map feature to discover nearby workplace opportunities:
- Location Detection: Uses browser or mobile GPS geolocation (with user consent) or accepts a manually entered street address.
- Adjustable Proximity Radius: Search radius options of 5 km, 10 km, 20 km, or 50 km (default radius is 10 km).
- Map Visualization: Pins verified office locations with distance calculations and quick preview cards linking directly to job details.

## AI Personalized Job Recommendations
Logged-in candidates receive tailored job opportunities matched to their specific profile and CV data:
- Match Scoring Engine: Evaluates alignment between candidate skills, declared work expectations, past project experience, and job requirements.
- Transparent Justification: Every recommended job post includes explicit reasoning, such as: "Matches 4 of 5 required technical skills: TypeScript, React, Next.js, and Tailwind CSS."
- Empty Profile Handling: If a new profile has not yet added skills or CVs, InterVue displays the most recent verified job listings accompanied by prompts to complete profile setup.

## Company Pages and Employer Profiles
Candidates can research prospective employers before submitting applications:
- Company Information: Overview, corporate mission, office gallery, website, tax code, and headquarters address.
- Trust Indicators: Official Verification Badges indicating verified business identity.
- Open Positions: Live listing of all currently active hiring postings by the organization.
- Follow Employer: Candidates can follow companies to receive notifications whenever new job openings are published.

## Job Alerts and Saved Postings
- Saved Jobs: Bookmark appealing job opportunities with one click to review, compare, and apply later from your dashboard.
- Job Alerts: Set up automated email digests for specific job titles and locations. Choose daily or weekly frequency. Every digest email includes an instant one-click unsubscribe link.
