# Application Tracking and Pipeline Statuses

## Application Pipeline Overview
InterVue provides complete transparency throughout the recruitment journey. Every submitted application progresses through a well-defined state machine with distinct statuses visible to both candidate and recruiter:

- `APPLIED` (New): The application has been submitted successfully and is queued in the employer's applicant pool.
- `IN_REVIEW` (In Review): The hiring team has opened your profile and is actively assessing your qualifications and screening responses. Candidates can see the exact date and timestamp when the recruiter first viewed their CV.
- `INTERVIEW` (Interview): The candidate has been shortlisted and invited to an interview session (accompanied by an interview schedule).
- `OFFER` (Offer Sent): The employer has extended a formal employment offer with detailed terms and compensation.
- `HIRED` (Hired): The candidate has accepted the offer and finalized hiring agreements. This is a positive terminal state.
- `REJECTED` (Rejected): The employer has decided not to proceed further. This is a terminal state.
- `WITHDRAWN` (Withdrawn by Candidate): The candidate voluntarily retracted their application before a final decision was made. This is a terminal state.
- `NO_AGREEMENT` / Post Closed: The job listing closed or expired before an offer was concluded, or both parties could not reach mutually agreeable terms.

## Tracking Dashboard and Visual Timeline
Candidates can track all their active and historical submissions from their Candidate Dashboard:
- Status Filtering: Filter applications by active stage (New, In Review, Interview, Offer) or past outcomes (Hired, Rejected, Withdrawn).
- Interactive Stepper: Visual progress indicators show where your dossier currently stands in the hiring pipeline.
- Audit Timeline: Detailed chronological logs document each status transition, interview invitation timestamp, and offer notification.
- "Recruiter Viewed" Transparency: The system explicitly notifies candidates as soon as an employer opens their CV for the first time.

## Withdrawing an Application
Candidates maintain full autonomy over their active applications:
- Withdrawal Eligibility: You can retract an application at any point as long as it has not yet reached a final terminal status (`HIRED`, `REJECTED`, or `WITHDRAWN`).
- Reason Feedback: Candidates may provide a brief optional reason for withdrawal (e.g., accepted another offer, relocated, or compensation mismatch) to help employers understand pipeline drops.
- Subsequent Reapplication: Withdrawing does not ban you from the company; you may reapply in the future if the position remains open.

## Real-Time Notification System
Whenever an employer updates the status of your application, InterVue alerts you through multiple channels:
- In-App Notifications: Instant alerts accessible from the top navigation bell icon and dashboard notification center.
- Email Notifications: Automated email updates sent immediately when you are moved to In Review, invited to an interview, or sent an offer letter.
