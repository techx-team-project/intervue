# Interview Scheduling and Candidate Confirmation

## Overview of the Interview Management Workflow
InterVue streamlines the interview coordination process between recruiters and candidates directly on the platform:
- Stage Transition: Once a candidate's application is reviewed and moved to the "Interview" stage, recruiters can schedule multiple interview rounds (Technical Interview, Cultural Fit, HR Round, Final Round).
- Transparent Timeline: Candidates and hiring teams share a synchronized schedule view with calendar details, meeting formats, and interview instructions.

## Interview Formats and Meeting Information
Recruiters configure interview details according to the job requirements:
- Online Video Interviews: Conducted via virtual meeting platforms such as Google Meet, Zoom, or Microsoft Teams. The invitation contains the direct meeting link and access code.
- In-Person / Offline Interviews: Conducted at the company's designated office address, complete with floor details, contact person, and arrival instructions.
- Duration and Date/Time: Specifies the exact date, start time, end time, and timezone (Vietnam Time GMT+7).

## Candidate Confirmation and Response Actions
When an interview is scheduled, candidates receive instant notifications and can respond from their dashboard or the interview detail page:
- Option 1: Confirm Attendance (Xác nhận tham gia): Accepts the scheduled time and confirms availability to the hiring team.
- Option 2: Request Reschedule (Yêu cầu dời lịch / đổi lịch): Proposes an alternate date and time along with a mandatory polite explanation for the schedule conflict. The recruiter can review the request and propose a new slot.
- Option 3: Decline Interview (Từ chối): Politely declines the interview with an optional reason if the candidate is no longer interested or has accepted another offer.

## Interview Status Tracking and Reminders
InterVue tracks each interview through clear status states:
- PENDING_CONFIRMATION: Awaiting the candidate's confirmation response.
- CONFIRMED: Both parties have confirmed attendance; automated reminder emails are sent 24 hours and 1 hour prior to the interview.
- RESCHEDULE_REQUESTED: Candidate submitted a request for an alternate interview time.
- DECLINED: Candidate declined the interview invitation.
- COMPLETED: The interview round has finished, and recruiters record feedback notes in the recruitment pipeline.
- CANCELLED: The interview was cancelled by the recruiter with an explanatory notice sent to the candidate.
