# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## Account, Identity, and Security

### Can I use the same email to both find jobs and hire candidates?
Yes. On InterVue, a single account identified by one email address can hold both Candidate and Recruiter roles (dùng một email cho cả 2 vai trò). You do not need to create two separate accounts; you can switch between your candidate job search dashboard and the recruiter management workspace seamlessly (chuyển đổi giữa các vai trò liền mạch).

### Why is citizen identity verification (eKYC) mandatory for candidates?
To eliminate fake profiles, ghost applicants, and automated spam, InterVue requires all candidates to complete eKYC with their official citizen identity card (CCCD) and biometric face scan before applying for jobs. This ensures employers that every applicant is an authentic, verified professional.

### What happens if my eKYC verification is rejected?
If your eKYC attempt is rejected, the system provides a specific explanation (such as blurry image, glare, or mismatched face scan) so you can retake the photos correctly. Candidates have up to 5 attempts. If you reach 5 unsuccessful attempts, you can submit a manual verification request to be reviewed by platform administrators within 24 working hours.

### How does Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) and account deletion work?
Users can enable 2FA using Google/Microsoft Authenticator (TOTP) or email OTP, along with 10 single-use emergency backup codes. Account deletion has a 7-day grace period; logging in during those 7 days automatically cancels deletion and restores access.

## Finding Jobs, Map Search, and Company Tracking

### How does the Jobs Near Me feature work?
Navigate to `/jobs/near-me` to explore technology job openings on an interactive map. You can allow browser GPS detection or select a custom location, then adjust the distance filter (5km, 10km, 25km, 50km) to discover nearby hiring tech offices.

### Can I save jobs and follow tech companies?
Yes. Click the bookmark icon on any job to save it under `/candidate/saved`. Click "Follow" on any company profile to receive email and in-app notifications whenever that employer publishes new job opportunities.

## Interview Coordination and Responses

### How do candidates respond to scheduled interviews?
When a recruiter schedules an interview round (Online via Google Meet/Teams or In-person at the office), the candidate receives an invitation notification. The candidate can:
1. Confirm attendance immediately.
2. Request a reschedule with a proposed alternate date and explanation.
3. Politely decline the invitation with an optional reason.

## Artificial Intelligence and Candidate Tools

### Does AI make automated decisions to accept or reject job applications?
No. On InterVue, AI serves strictly as an intelligent assistant to summarize relevant experience and score compatibility. Final hiring decisions to interview, hire, or reject applicants are made exclusively by human recruiters.

### How does AI update my profile from a CV?
When you upload or select a PDF CV in the profile manager, AI extracts your headline, bio, experience, skills, education, certifications, and languages. If your profile is empty, it pre-fills everything for one-click confirmation. If your profile already has content, it displays an Old vs New comparison per field so you selectively choose what to update.

### What happens if AI cannot read my CV?
If your CV is an image-only scanned PDF without digital text (OCR), password-protected, or corrupted, the system detects the unreadable reason, displays a clear explanation, and provides a direct shortcut to enter your profile manually.

### How do AI Job Recommendations work if my profile is empty?
If your profile has complete skills and experience, AI recommends matching jobs with a short one-sentence explanation for each. If your profile is empty, the system displays the newest trending tech opportunities accompanied by an encouraging nudge banner prompting you to complete your profile.

### How do I use AI to write a Cover Letter?
When viewing an active job post, candidates can click "Draft Cover Letter" or ask the AI Chatbot to draft one. AI analyzes the job's technical requirements against your default CV to produce a personalized, professional application letter that you can review and edit before sending.

## Platform Limits and Support

### What are the query limits for the InterVue Support Chatbot?
The AI support assistant provides guidance on navigating and using the InterVue platform. The following rate limits apply:
- Unregistered Guests: Up to 10 questions per hour, tracked by IP address.
- Logged-in Users: Up to 30 questions per day across the platform.

### How can I report a fraudulent job post or employer?
If you encounter a suspicious job listing, fraudulent employer, or request for unauthorized application fees, click the "Report" button on the listing. Platform administrators review and act on all reports promptly.
