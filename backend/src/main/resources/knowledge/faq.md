# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## Account and Identity Questions

### Can I use the same email to both find jobs and hire candidates?
Yes. On InterVue, a single account identified by one email address can hold both Candidate and Recruiter roles. You do not need to create two separate accounts; you can switch between your candidate job search dashboard and the recruiter management workspace seamlessly.

### Why is citizen identity verification (eKYC) mandatory for candidates?
To eliminate fake profiles, ghost applicants, and automated spam, InterVue requires all candidates to complete eKYC with their official citizen identity card (CCCD) and biometric face scan before applying for jobs. This ensures employers that every applicant is an authentic, verified professional.

### What happens if my eKYC verification is rejected?
If your eKYC attempt is rejected, the system provides a specific explanation (such as blurry image, glare, or mismatched face scan) so you can retake the photos correctly. Candidates have up to 5 attempts. If you reach 5 unsuccessful attempts, you can submit a manual verification request to be reviewed by platform administrators within 24 working hours.

## Recruitment and AI Questions

### Does AI make automated decisions to accept or reject job applications?
No. On InterVue, AI serves strictly as an intelligent assistant to summarize relevant experience and score compatibility. Final hiring decisions to interview, hire, or reject applicants are made exclusively by human recruiters.

### How does the AI Assistant help candidates?
AI assists candidates by providing multi-level CV audits, recommending compatible job listings based on verified skills, generating personalized cover letter drafts, and conducting interactive mock technical interviews with instant feedback.

### Are job applications and recruiter messaging free for candidates?
Yes. Browsing job postings, submitting applications, and messaging recruiters through the built-in chat system are 100% free for candidates.

## Platform Limits and Payment Questions

### What are the query limits for the InterVue Support Chatbot?
The AI support assistant provides guidance on navigating and using the InterVue platform. To ensure fair access and prevent automated abuse, the following rate limits apply:
- Unregistered Guests: Up to 10 questions per hour, tracked by IP address.
- Logged-in Users: Up to 30 questions per day across the platform.

### How are payments handled for paid services?
Payments for optional premium tiers (such as Pro plans or Level 3 CV compatibility checks) are processed via bank transfer powered by SePay:
- Instant QR Code: When you initiate a purchase, an order is generated with an exact payment QR code valid for 30 minutes.
- Automated Reconciliation: InterVue monitors incoming transactions via an automated background worker. Once payment is confirmed, service credits or subscriptions activate immediately.
- Idempotency and Safety: Transactions are processed strictly once to avoid duplicate charges.

### How can I report a fraudulent job post or scam?
If you encounter a suspicious job listing, fraudulent employer, or request for unauthorized application fees, click the "Report" button on the listing. Provide a clear description and optional screenshots. Platform administrators review and act on all reports promptly.
