# Interview Scheduling and AI Mock Interviews

## Managing Recruiter Interview Invitations
When an employer shortlists your profile, they schedule an official interview session through the InterVue platform:
- Invitation Details: Each interview invitation clearly specifies the scheduled date and time, local timezone, expected duration, and interview format:
  - Online Video Meeting: Includes meeting platform link (Google Meet, Zoom, or Microsoft Teams) and dial-in instructions.
  - Offline In-Person Interview: Details the physical office address, floor, building name, and check-in instructions.
- Preparation Notes: Recruiters often include guidance on interview format (e.g., live coding challenge, system design discussion, or culture fit interview) and lists of required documents.

## Candidate Actions on Interview Requests
Upon receiving an interview invitation, candidates can choose from three actions:
1. Accept: Confirms attendance and locks the timeslot on both parties' dashboards.
2. Decline: Gracefully declines the interview invitation with an optional polite note.
3. Propose Reschedule: Suggests alternative dates and time windows if the proposed slot conflicts with your schedule.

Calendar Integration: Once confirmed, you can sync the interview directly to your Google Calendar or download an `.ics` file with one click.

## Automated Interview Reminders
To ensure candidates never miss an appointment, InterVue sends automated reminders:
- 24-Hour Notice: An email and in-app alert sent 24 hours prior to the scheduled meeting.
- 1-Hour Reminder: An urgent notification sent 1 hour before start time containing the direct meeting link or office directions.

## AI Mock Interview Practice
InterVue offers an intelligent automated practice environment to help developers polish their technical interviewing skills before talking to real employers:
- Entry Points:
  - General Practice: Select your CV and target role from the Mock Interview dashboard.
  - Job-Specific Practice: Click "Practice Interview for this Job" on any job posting page to generate questions tailored to the company's specific tech stack.
- Format and Questions: Text-based conversation consisting of 5 to 10 tailored technical and behavioral questions per session. AI may ask a single follow-up probe if your response lacks necessary architectural or implementation depth.
- Instant Turn-by-Turn Feedback: After every answer, the AI analyzes technical correctness, conceptual depth, clarity of communication, and suggests optimal answer frameworks.
- Comprehensive Performance Report: At the conclusion of the session, the AI provides a summary highlighting your top 3 standout strengths and top 3 actionable areas for improvement.
- Absolute Privacy: Mock interview transcripts and scores are strictly confidential to the candidate. They are never shared with recruiters or factored into platform search rankings.
