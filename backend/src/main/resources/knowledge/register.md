# Registration and Account Creation on InterVue

## Overview of Account Types and Roles
InterVue is a specialized IT recruitment platform connecting tech talent with employers. The platform supports three primary user roles:
- Candidate (Job seekers and IT interns)
- Recruiter (HR professionals and corporate hiring representatives)
- Administrator (Platform operators managing verifications and moderation)

A single user account identified by a unique email address can hold both Candidate and Recruiter roles simultaneously. There is no need to register separate email addresses to switch between seeking jobs and recruiting candidates.

## Registration Methods
Users can register an account on InterVue through two main methods:
1. Social Authentication: One-click sign-up using Google, GitHub, Facebook, or LinkedIn.
2. Email Registration Form: Sign-up using standard full name, email, and password credentials.

When signing up via social providers, InterVue retrieves your verified email address from the provider. If the email already exists on InterVue with verified status, the system automatically links the social provider to the existing account and sends a security confirmation email.

## Email Registration Requirements
When registering with an email address, users must complete the following fields:
- Full Name: Display name used across applications and platform interactions.
- Email Address: Must be unique across the platform.
- Password and Confirm Password: Must follow strict password security rules.
- Policy Agreement: Explicit checkbox consent to the InterVue Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
- Minimum Age Confirmation: Explicit checkbox confirming the user is at least 16 years old.

The 16-year-old minimum age requirement is strictly enforced and is re-verified against the date of birth extracted from official identity documents during candidate eKYC.

## Password Security Guidelines
Passwords created on InterVue must satisfy the following security rules:
- Minimum length of 8 characters.
- Must include at least one uppercase letter (A-Z).
- Must include at least one lowercase letter (a-z).
- Must include at least one numerical digit (0-9).
- Must include at least one special character (such as @, #, $, %, !, &, *).
- The password must not match the account email address.

## Email Verification Process
After submitting the email registration form, InterVue sends an activation email containing a secure verification link:
- Verification link validity: The link expires in 24 hours and can only be used once.
- Resend cooldown: Users must wait 60 seconds between resend requests.
- Rate limits: A maximum of 3 verification emails can be requested within a 15-minute window per email/IP.
- Unverified account restrictions: Users with unverified emails can log in and browse public job posts, but cannot initiate electronic identity verification (eKYC) or submit job applications.
