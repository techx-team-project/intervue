# Candidate Profile and Identity Verification

## Candidate Profile Overview
The InterVue candidate profile serves as your comprehensive professional portfolio for tech employers. It showcases your career background, verified technical skills, highlighted projects, education, and job expectations. Completing your profile increases your visibility in recruiter candidate searches and unlocks personalized AI job recommendations.

## Basic Information and Privacy Controls
Candidates can customize their profile with the following personal information:
- Profile picture and cover image.
- Full name and professional headline (e.g., Senior Backend Engineer).
- Job search status: Actively Looking, Open to Opportunities, or Not Looking.
- Contact email and phone number: Visible exclusively to recruiters of jobs you have applied to, or after accepting a direct chat invitation.
- Living location (city and province).
- Portfolio website, GitHub, and LinkedIn profile links.

InterVue prioritizes candidate privacy:
- Birth year and gender are optional and hidden from recruiters by default.
- AI evaluation algorithms do not consider age, gender, photo, or birthplace.
- Candidates can toggle the "Allow recruiters to find me" switch to control search visibility.

## Professional Experience and Skills
Your career history and technical capabilities are organized into structured sections:
- Work Experience: Company name, job title, start and end dates (or current position), key responsibilities, notable achievements, and technologies used.
- Standardized Technical Skills: Skills are selected from a standardized catalog categorized into Core Skills, AI & Data, DevOps & Tools, and Soft Skills. Each skill includes proficiency level (Basic, Intermediate, Proficient, Expert) and years of experience.
- Featured Projects: Project name, role, tech stack, duration, summary of impact, demo URL, and GitHub repository links.
- Education and Credentials: Academic degrees, university, graduation year, GPA, academic honors, and uploaded certifications with verification links.

## Career Expectations
To receive relevant job matches, candidates must configure their job preferences:
- Desired Position: Mandatory field specifying target technical roles.
- Target Job Level: Intern, Fresher, Junior, Middle, Senior, Lead, or Manager.
- Expected Salary: Specified monthly salary range (minimum to maximum) or marked as negotiable.
- Working Mode: On-site, Hybrid, Remote, or Flexible.
- Employment Type: Full-time, Part-time, or Internship.
- Preferred Locations: Selected cities where you are willing to work.

## AI Profile Autofill from CV
Candidates can save time by letting AI automatically populate their profile from an uploaded CV:
- Empty Profile: AI extracts information across all sections for one-click candidate review and approval.
- Existing Profile: AI presents side-by-side comparative diffs showing current versus suggested entries. Candidates choose specific sections to update without accidental overwrites.
- Unreadable files: If the CV cannot be parsed cleanly, the system highlights the issue and allows manual entry.

## Mandatory Identity Verification (eKYC)
To build mutual trust and eliminate spam and fraudulent applications, all candidates must complete electronic identity verification (eKYC) before applying for any job on InterVue.
- Prerequisite: Email address must be verified.
- Verification Partner: Powered by FPT.AI using official Vietnamese Citizen Identity Cards (CCCD/CMND).
- Steps: Upload front side of CCCD, upload back side of CCCD, and complete biometric face liveness scanning.
- Automatic Approval: Approved automatically if OCR extraction, face matching, and liveness scores are all 80% or higher.
- Manual Admin Review: Requests with confidence scores between 50% and 80%, or duplicate citizen ID records, are routed to platform administrators for review within 24 working hours.
- Rejection Rules: Scores of 50% or below, or extracted birth dates under 16 years of age, are rejected automatically. Candidates are permitted up to 5 attempts before requiring manual administrator assistance.
