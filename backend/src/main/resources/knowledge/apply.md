# Job Application Process and Guidelines

## Application Eligibility and Prerequisites
Before submitting an application on InterVue, candidates must meet the following criteria:
1. Verified Account Status (eKYC): The candidate's account must have completed electronic identity verification with a verified citizen identity card (CCCD) and biometric liveness check. Unverified accounts cannot submit applications.
2. Active Listing: The job listing must be in open status and actively accepting candidate dossiers. Expired or paused postings cannot receive submissions.
3. Conflict of Interest Protection: Recruiters are strictly prohibited from applying to jobs published by their own company account.
4. Single Active Application Limit: A candidate may have only one active application per job post at any given time.

## Application Form and Submission Options
Employers configure the application format based on the role's requirements:
- Standard CV Submission: Select your preferred uploaded PDF CV or upload a new CV to attach to the application.
- Screening Questions: Employers may include pre-screening questionnaires consisting of multiple-choice or short written technical questions (up to 5 questions).
  - Candidates are informed of the total question count and estimated completion time before beginning.
  - Answers are automatically saved as drafts so progress is never lost if interrupted.
  - Full review screen allows reviewing all responses prior to final submission.

## AI Cover Letter Assistant
Candidates can craft professional, targeted cover letters directly inside the application dialog:
- One-Click AI Generation: Click "Generate with AI" to produce a customized draft tailored to the specific job description and your selected CV.
- Honest Alignment: The AI analyzes genuine overlapping strengths, highlighting matching technologies and relevant project experience.
- Integrity Guard: The AI assistant strictly draws from existing CV entries and never invents unverified achievements or fictitious skills.
- Mandatory Review: The generated text is placed in an editable input box for the candidate to review, personalize, and approve before sending. No letter is ever sent automatically.

## Reapplication Rules
If a candidate withdraws an earlier submission, they are permitted to reapply for the same job posting while it remains active:
- Screening Answer Retention: The answers provided to screening questions during the initial submission are preserved and cannot be altered upon reapplication.
- CV Updating: Candidates are allowed to attach an updated or different CV when reapplying.

## Candidate-Recruiter Communication
- Realtime WebSocket Chat: Integrated messaging allows seamless real-time communication between candidates and hiring managers once an application is active.
- Completely Free for Candidates: Job seekers are never charged any fee to message recruiters or reply to interview inquiries.
- Direct Outreach Invitations: Recruiters browsing candidate profiles can initiate contact via a message invitation. If the candidate accepts, direct chat unlocks. To prevent spam, recruiters are limited to 1 invitation per candidate every 30 days.
